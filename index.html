How the Cup works
Teams

Every player goes on one of two wheels — forwards and defenders. Each spin pairs one from each wheel, and that pairing is your team for the whole cup. The wheel order is random, and nobody is drafted twice.

Scoring a match
  • Every goal is entered against the player who scored it — two boxes per team, forward then defender, in the same order the team name reads.
  • A team's score is simply its two players added up, and the higher total wins. There is no team-score box.
  • One box per team — the goals that team scored, and the higher total wins. Who scored them isn't recorded this cup; individual goals start with the next one.
  • There are no draws — equal totals are rejected, not recorded.
  • A match counts for nothing until all four boxes are in. Someone who didn't score gets a 0, not an empty box.
  • A match counts for nothing until both boxes are in.
  • The knockout is scored exactly the same way. Nothing is decided by tapping a team.
Deadlines

Every match in the live stage is due 2 working days after that stage opens — the group stage from the draft, the knockout from the moment it starts. Weekends don't count, so a Thursday draft is due Monday. The banner at the top counts down to it.

  • Play whenever both teams are in the office — there are no fixed slots.
  • A no-show is set with the forfeit picker under the match, not with the goal boxes — nobody scored, so there is no player to hang a goal on. A forfeited match counts towards nobody's Golden Boot — though the 1-0 does count against the loser's Golden Glove.
  • A team that doesn't turn up by the deadline forfeits 1-0. It's recorded as 1-0 rather than 10-0 so an unplayed match can never outrank a real result on goal difference.
  • If neither team turns up, the group match goes down as 0-0. It counts for nobody: no win, no goals, and neither team is even marked as having played it. A real match can't finish 0-0 — someone gets to 10 — so that score means "nobody came", not "a draw".
  • In the knockout a 0-0 isn't possible, because somebody has to go through — so there the picker only offers the two one-sided no-shows. If neither team turns up, the Match Toss decides it and the loser is forfeited through.
  • No substitutes. Your drafted pair is your team for the whole cup.
The table

There is no points system. Teams are ranked directly by results:

  • P — matches played  ·  W — matches won
  • GD — goal difference (goals scored minus conceded)
  • GF — goals scored

Order is decided by wins first, then GD, then GF. If two teams are still exactly level, whoever won the match between them goes above. The highlighted rows are the ones who go through.

Format

There is always one group — every team plays every other team once, whatever the team count. How many go through depends on the size:

  • 2 teams — no group stage at all, straight to the Grand Final.
  • 3 or 4 teams — top two meet in the Grand Final.
  • 5 to 9 teams — top four reach the Semifinals, seeded 1 v 4 and 2 v 3.
  • 10 or more — top eight reach the Quarterfinals, seeded 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 2 v 7 and 3 v 6.

Seeding is crossed at every size, so the teams who topped the group meet as late as the bracket allows.

Suggesting a score

Played a group match while the admin wasn't around? Sign in with any Google account and type each player's goalsthe score straight into that match — it is sent as a suggestion, shown to everyone with your name on it. It doesn't change the standings; the admin accepts it (or corrects it) when they're back. Only matches with no score yet can be suggested.

When the knockout starts

The Start Knockout button appears only once every group match is settled — a score, a forfeit, or a 0-0 — so there are no partial brackets and no single unplayed match can hold the cup shut. The admin starts it, and from that point the group scores are locked, so the table behind the bracket can't shift.

Deciding a knockout match
  • Knockout ties are scored like every other match — four goal boxesa score a side, higher total goes through. Nobody is tapped through.
  • Changing an earlier round clears everything after it, scores included, since those matchups no longer exist.

Winning the Grand Final takes the cup, and that team is added to the Hall of Fame the moment the final is decided.

Golden Boot & Golden Glove
  • Golden Boot — the most individual goals in the cup, group and knockout together. The race is live under the group table all cup long.
  • Golden Glove — the fewest goals conceded per match, and only the defence is in the running. Goals against are a team total, shared by both partners, so opening it up could only ever name a pair.
  • Per match, not in total: everyone plays the same group fixtures, but reaching the final means two or three knockouts on top. On a raw total those extra matches would just be extra goals against — going deep would cost you the Glove.
  • Level on the rate? It goes to whoever held it over more matches.
  • Both are shared when players are still level — no arbitrary tie-break.
  • A forfeit credits no goalscorer — there's no breakdown behind it — but the 1-0 still concedes. Not turning up costs your Glove.
